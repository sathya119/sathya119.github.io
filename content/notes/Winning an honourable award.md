This is a recurring daydream, and has stuck with me ever since I have heard about an honour like the Bharat Ratna or a Nobel Prize.

>Now dear reader, do not act like you have never daydreamed about this. You would have AT LEAST dreamt about this moment in your sleep ;)

Most of the time, it is about how I would receive the honour, what would I speak, how would I address the crowd, whom will I thank, with whom will I click the first picture, etc.
But sometimes, (almost every time nowadays), it is about what I would wear, whom will I invite with me and whose reaction I look forward to the most. The last one has sadistic and benign thoughts behind but we shan’t dive deep into them so that I can maintain my image :)

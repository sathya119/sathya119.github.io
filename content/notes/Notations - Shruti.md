You would come across two broad classifications of Shruti notations:
	1. <u>Western</u>: This system follows the western notation of notes (C, D, E, etc.).
	2. <u>Indian</u>: This system follows a numerical notation starting with 1 (C) up to 7 (B).

You would also, very often come across an alphabet next to the notation, either P or M. It stands for Panchama and Madhyama respectively.

The drone used for Shruti, usually a [[Tanpura]], has 3 different swaras to which it is tuned to: Lower Sa, Upper Sa, and Panchama or Madhyama.

>Check [[What is a Swara]] to know what Panchama and Madhyama are.

For example, 2P would mean that the drone is tuned to D G and upper D. As you see, it is helpful when you know both systems of notations to actually understand what Shruti means.

The visual system is the part of the central nervous system that give organisms the ability to detect, process and interpret information from visual light.

Process:
1. Reception by Retina
2. Transmission by Optic Nerve to Thalamus (relay station)
3. Storage and Processing in Thalamus
4. Information transport to Visual Cortex
5. Visual Cortex extracts shape, colour, object identity, distance, motion, etc.

The information flow in Cortex is both Sequential (ordered like a series of digits) and Parallel (multiple streams of information like colour, shape and texture).

1. **Poincaré Invariance:**
	
	$$\tilde{X}^\mu=\Lambda_{\nu}^\mu X^\nu+a^\mu$$
	
	$$\tilde{h}^{ab}=h^{ab}$$
	
	where $$\Lambda^\mu_{\nu}\Lambda^\rho_{\sigma}\eta_{\mu\rho}=\eta_{\nu\sigma}$$. The generators $$\Lambda_{\nu}^\mu,\;b^\mu$$ are independent of the world-sheet coordinates $$\sigma^a$$.

2. **Reparametrisation Invariance:**
	
	Consider coordinate transformations $$\sigma^a\rightarrow \tilde{\sigma}^a(\sigma)$$. Then,
	
	$$\tilde{X}^\mu(\sigma)=X^\mu(\sigma)$$
	
	$$\frac{\partial \tilde{\sigma}^c}{\partial\sigma^a}\frac{\partial \tilde{\sigma}^d}{\partial\sigma^b}\tilde{h}_{cd}(\tilde{\sigma})=h_{ab}(\sigma)$$.

3. **Weyl Scaling**
	
	$$\tilde{X}^\mu(\sigma)=X^\mu(\sigma)$$
	
	$$\tilde{h}^{ab}(\sigma)=e^{\Theta(\sigma)}h^{ab}(\sigma)$$
	
	where $$\Theta(\sigma)$$ is an arbitrary function.

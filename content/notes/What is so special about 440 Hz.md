440 Hz is called the Standard Concert Pitch and is used as a reference to tune musical instruments.

Why? There are historical reasons for why 440 Hz was chosen as an international standard but it was done so to maintain a globally consistent tuning.

Although, a sidenote, some people follow Verdi tuning, in which 432 Hz is set as the reference. Why? From what I have heard, 432 Hz aligns with natural vibrations in the universe, particularly the Schumann resonance of the Earth. This would lead to a more harmonious and potentially beneficial listening experience than the standard 440 Hz tuning.

Some other tuning standards are 415 Hz and 466 Hz.

- [[The Two Higgs Doublet Model]]

The Higgs sector of the SM is highly predictive. The predictions include

- Coincidence of the cubic and quartic Higgs couplings $λ_3$ and $λ_4$
- The absence of flavour-changing neutral scalar currents.

Both of the above are possible in a variety of BSM models. We have seen explicitly that in the 2HDMs, flavour-changing currents can emerge. We have also seen that generic (model-independent) statements can be made for the ratio of $λ_3$ and $λ_4$, which is highly constraining e.g. for the coefficients of the SMEFT.

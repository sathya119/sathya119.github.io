We use the following shorthand notation:

$$\dot{X}^\mu=\partial_{\tau}X^\mu\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;X'^\mu=\partial_{\sigma}X^\mu$$

such that the gauge-fixed Polyakov action is 

$$S=\int d\tau\;\mathcal{L}=\frac{T}{2}\int^\pi_{0}d\sigma\;\left[\dot{X}^2+X'^2\right]$$.

The world-sheet canonical momenta are:

$$\Pi_{\mu}=\frac{\delta\mathcal{L}}{\delta \dot{X}^\mu}=T\dot{X}^\mu$$.

Then, the gauge-fixed action becomes (canonical form of the action)

$$S=\int d\tau\;\left[\int^\pi_{0}d\sigma\;\dot{X}^\mu \Pi_{\mu}-H\right]$$

with Hamiltonian 

$$H=\frac{1}{2}\int^\pi_{0}d\sigma\;\left[\frac{\Pi^2}{T}+TX'^2\right]=\frac{1}{2}\int^\pi_{0}d\sigma(\dot{X}^2+X'^2)$$.

Thus, 

$$\{X^\mu(\sigma),\Pi_{\nu}(\sigma')\}=\delta(\sigma-\sigma')\delta^\mu_{\nu}$$ with all other Poisson brackets vanishing.

The Poisson brackets for the coefficients in the mode expansion are 

$$\{x^\mu,p_{\nu}\}=\delta^\mu_{\nu}$$

$$\{\alpha^\mu_{n},\alpha^\nu_{n}\}=-im\delta_{m+n}\eta^{\mu \nu}$$

$$\{\tilde{\alpha}^\mu_{n},\tilde{\alpha}^\nu_{n}\}=-im\delta_{m+n}\eta^{\mu \nu}$$

with all other Poisson brackets vanishing.

Goal - To construct a gauge invariant SU(Nc) QFT for quarks (fermionic matter fields) and gluons (massless gauge fields).

[[Colour Algebra]]

The final Lagrangian can be written as 

$$\mathcal{L}_{QCD}=\mathcal{L}_{classical}+\mathcal{L}_{fix}+\mathcal{L}_{ghost}$$

where the first piece contains all potential terms and interaction terms, second piece enables us to fix a gluon propagator by (partly) fixing a gauge and the third piece contains Fadeev-Popov ghost terms that arise from our gauge fixing procedure.

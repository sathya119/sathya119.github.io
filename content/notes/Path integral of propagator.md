Now, a kernel can be written as
$$K(x_B,t_B;x_A,t_A)=\int dx_1\int dx_2...\int dx_N K(x_B,t_B;x_1,t_1)K(x_1,t_1;x_2,t_2)...K(x_N,t_N;x_A,t_A)$$

On finding an expression for t=0, we find 

$$\int_{t_A}^{t_B}dt[\frac{m\dot{x}(t)^2}{2}-V(x(t))]=S[x]$$

Hence, 

$$K(x_B,t_B;x_A,t_A)=\int Dx(t)e^{\frac{i}{\hbar}S[x]}$$

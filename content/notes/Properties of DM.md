**1. Non-Baryonic**
	Results of the CMB, together with the predictions from Big Bang nucleosynthesis, suggest that only 4-5% of the total energy is made out of ordinary, baryonic matter. Hence, we must conclude that DM is non-baryonic.
	<u>Neutrinos</u> - A special mention because they are the only viable non-baryonic DM candidate with a place in the Standard Model. Neutrinos are very abundant particles in the universe with a (very small) mass. They also interact feebly with ordinary matter (only through electro-weak interactions). Hence, they are in fact, a component of DM. But,
		1. Neutrinos are too light.
		2. Neutrinos are relativistic (hot) at the epoch of structure formation.

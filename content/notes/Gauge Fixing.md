Locally, we set the world-sheet metric to any convenient constant form by a gauge transformation. The simplest choice is 

$$h_{ab}=\eta_{ab}:=\begin{pmatrix} -1&0\\0&1 \end{pmatrix} $$.

The action then simplifies to 

$$S=-\frac{T}{2}\int_{\Sigma}d^2\sigma\;\eta^{ab}\partial_{a}X^\mu\partial_{b}X_{\mu}=-\frac{T}{2}\int_{\Sigma}d^2\sigma\;(\partial_{0}X^\mu\partial_{0}X_{\mu}-\partial_{1}X^\mu\partial_{1}X_{\mu})$$.

In this gauge, the coordinate functions X$^\mu(\sigma)$ are free fields in two dimensions.

The equation of motion is then the wave equation $$(\partial_{0}^2-\partial_{1}^2)X^\mu=0$$ with a general solution

$$X^\mu(\sigma)=X_{L}^\mu(\sigma^0+\sigma^1)+X_{R}^\mu(\sigma^0-\sigma^1)$$

for arbitrary functions X$_L$ and X$_R$. The subscripts refer to the left-moving and right-moving wave solutions respectively.

We now introduce light-cone coordinates as $$\sigma^\pm:=\sigma^0\pm\sigma^1$$, where the metric components are

$$\eta_{+-}=\eta_{-+}=-\frac{1}{2}\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\eta_{++}=\eta_{--}=0$$

$$\eta^{+-}=\eta^{-+}=-2\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\eta^{++}=\eta^{--}=0$$

The partial derivatives are given by 

$$\partial_{\pm}=\frac{1}{2}(\partial_{0}\pm\partial_{1})$$ and the wave operator is $$\partial_{0}^2-\partial_{1}^2=4\partial_{+}\partial_{-}$$.

The Polyakov action becomes 

$$S=T\int_{\Sigma}d^2\sigma\;\partial_{+}X^\mu\partial_{-}X_{\mu}$$.

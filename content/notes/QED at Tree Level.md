	$$i\mathcal{M}=\bar{u}_\alpha(p_1,s^,)(-ie)\gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}u_\beta(k_1,s)\frac{-i(g_{\mu\nu}-(\xi-1\frac{q_\mu q_\nu}{q^2}))}{q^2+i\epsilon}\bar{u}_\gamma(p_2,r^,)(-ie)\gamma^\nu_{\gamma\delta}u_\delta(k_2,r)$$ where the middle term denotes the photon propagator with the electron and muon lines to its left and right respectively.
	Observe that $$\bar{u}(p_1)\not qu(k_1) = 0\text{ since q = k1 - p1 = k2 - p2}$$
	To further simplify our result, we will choose the Feynman Gauge (ξ = 1) and drop the +iϵ from the propagators:$$i\mathcal{M}=ie^2\bar u(p_1,s^,)\gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}u_\beta(k_1,s)\frac{1}{q^2}\bar u_\gamma(p_2,r^,)\gamma_{\gamma\delta ,\nu}u_\delta(k_2,r)$$
	Now, to compute the cross-section, we use the following identity:$$[\bar u(p_1)\gamma^{\mu_1}...\gamma^{\mu_n}u(p_2)]^\dagger=\bar u(p_2)\gamma^{\mu_n}...\gamma^{\mu_1}u(p_1)$$
	So, $$|\mathcal{M}|^2 = \mathcal{M}.\mathcal{M}^*=\frac{e^4}{q^4}[\bar u(p_1)\gamma^\mu u(k_1)\bar u(p_2)\gamma_\mu u(k_2)].[\bar u(k_2)\gamma_{\mu^,} u(p_2)\bar u(k_1)\gamma^{\mu^,} u(p_1)]$$

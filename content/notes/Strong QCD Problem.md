The most general gauge invariant QCD Lagrangian upto dimension four reads

$$\mathcal{L}_{QCD}=-\frac{1}{4}G^a_{\mu\nu}G^{a,\mu\nu}+\bar{q}(i\gamma_\mu D^\mu-\mathcal M_q)q-\frac{\alpha_S}{8\pi}\theta \tilde G^a_{\mu\nu}\tilde G^{a,\mu\nu}$$

The zero temperature mass of the axion field is determined by non-perturbative QCD effects, related to the axion scale $$f_a$$ and can be computed as 

$$m_a(T=0)=5.7\times 10^{-5}\frac{10^{-11}}{f_a}$$.

The axion potential is periodic, but we can adopt a harmonic approximation as

$$V(\theta)=m_a^2(T)(1-cos\theta)\approx\frac{1}{2}m_a^2(T)\theta^2$$.

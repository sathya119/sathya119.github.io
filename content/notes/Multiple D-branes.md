Consider the T-dual perspective. The $ij$-strings now satisfy the boundary conditions

$$X'^d(\tau,\pi)-X'^d(\tau,0)=2\pi \alpha' \frac{\theta_{i}-\theta_{j}+2\pi n}{2\pi R}=(\theta_{i}-\theta_{j}+2\pi n)R'$$.

Thus, there are now $N$ independent D-branes situated at positions $x^d=R'\theta_{i}$ on the dual circle, up to a common additive constant. In the T-dual picture, $ij$-strings satisfy Dirichlet boundary conditions at both ends:

- Endpoint $\sigma=0$ ending on the D-brane at position $x^d=R'\theta_{i}$.
- Endpoint $\sigma=\pi$ ending on the D-brane at position $x^d=R'\theta_{j}$.

The mass-shell condition is now

$$m^{2}=\left( \frac{\theta_{i}-\theta_{j}+2\pi n}{2\pi R} \right)^{2}+\frac{2}{\alpha'}(N-a)$$.

In the original perspective, this simply reflects the shift in the contribution from the KK momentum. In T-dual perspective, the shift reflects additional contributions to the potential energy of $ij$-strings stretching between separated D-branes.

Let us look at the massless sector. Assuming the angles $\theta_{1},\dots,\theta_{N}$ are all distinct, the only massless states correspond to $i=j$ and $N=1$. In the original picture they are fluctuations of gauge field for the gauge symmetry $U(1)^N ⊂ U(N)$ left unbroken by the Wilson line. From the T-dual perspective, the D-branes are separated and the massless states have the following interpretation:

- Massless states $\alpha^m_{-1}\ket{0;k;ii}$ correspond to fluctuations of a $U(1)$ gauge field $(A_{m})_{ii}$ on the D-brane at $x^d=\theta_{i}R$.
- Massless states $\alpha_{-1}^d\ket{0;k;ii}$ correspond to fluctuations of a scalar field $(A_{d})_{ii}$ on the D-brane at $x^d=\theta_{i}R$.

A constant gauge connection $(A_{d})_{ii}=-\theta_{i}/2\pi R$ corresponds in the T-dual picture to translating the $i$-th parallel D-brane by $R'\theta_{i}$. It is therefore reasonable that fluctuations of $(A_{d})_{ii}$ correspond to fluctuations in the position of the $i$-th D-brane. Therefore, D-branes are dynamical objects.

In the limit that all $\theta_j$ coincide, the $U(N)$ gauge symmetry in the original picture is restored and in the T-dual picture, there are $N$ coincident D-branes. Thus, $N$ coincident D-branes support a $U(N)$ gauge theory with connection $(A_{m})_{ii}$. The remaining $N^2$ scalar fields $(A_{d})_{ii}$ are more mysterious.

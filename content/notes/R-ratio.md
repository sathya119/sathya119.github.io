R is the ratio of the hadronic cross-section to the muon cross-section in electron-positron collisions.

$$R=\frac{\sigma^{(0)}(e^+e^-\rightarrow hadrons)}{\sigma(e^+e^-\rightarrow\mu^+\mu^-)}$$

where superscript (0) indicates that the cross-section has been corrected for initial-state radiation. 

In QCD, $$R=3\sum_q\frac{e^2_q}{e^2}$$ provides experimental confirmation for the electric charge of quarks.

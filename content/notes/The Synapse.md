# Terminologies

- Polarity - In Neuroscience, polarity can have two different meaning with respect to Neurons.
	- They have a polarised (charged) membrane due to a difference in electrical potentials (voltage) within and outside the cell.
	- Typically, neurons are considered to be polarised since the two ends of the cell - dendrites and axon, specialise in different actions; receiving inputs from and sending outputs to other neurons respectively.

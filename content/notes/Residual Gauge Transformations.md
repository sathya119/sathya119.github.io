In light-cone quantisation, we used world-sheet re-parametrisation invariance and Weyl invariance to set $h_{ab}=\eta_{ab}$. However, this is only a partial gauge fixing, because there exists combinations of re-parametrisations and Weyl transformations, leaving this choice invariant. What are these residual gauge transformations?

The infinitesimal re-parametrisations $\delta \sigma^a+\xi^a$ of the world-sheet coordinates transform the world-sheet metric by

$$\delta h^{ab}=\xi^c\partial_{c}h^{ab}-h^{ac}\partial_{c}\xi^b-h^{bc}\partial_{c}\xi^a$$.

Thus, setting $h_{ab}=\eta_{ab}$, we find $\delta \eta^{ab}=-(\partial^a\xi^b+\partial^b\xi^a)$. This transformation must be compensated by an infinitesimal Weyl transformation $\delta \eta^{ab}=\Theta \eta^{ab}$. Thus, these residual gauge transformations are solutions to

$$\partial^a\xi^b+\partial^b\xi^a=\Theta \eta^{ab}$$.

In the light-cone coordinates,

$$\partial_{+}\xi^-=\partial_{-}\xi^+=0$$

$$\partial_{+}\xi^++\partial_{-}\xi^-=\Theta$$.

The general solution is given by arbitrary re-parametrisations of the form

$$\xi^+=\xi^+(\sigma^+)\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\xi^-=\xi^-(\sigma^-)$$

combined with a Weyl transformation by $\Theta=\partial_{+}\xi^++\partial_{-}\xi^-$.

Exponentiating, finite-residual gauge transformations correspond to re-parametrisations of the form

$$\sigma^+\to \tilde{\sigma}^+=f^+(\sigma^+)\;\;\;\;\;\;\sigma^-\to \tilde{\sigma}^-=f^-(\sigma^-)$$.

In terms of the original coordinates $\tau$ and $\sigma$, this is

$$\tau\to \tilde{\tau}=\frac{1}{2}(f^+(\tau+\sigma)+f^-(\tau-\sigma))$$

$$\sigma \to \tilde{\sigma}=\frac{1}{2}(f^+(\tau+\sigma)-f^-(\tau-\sigma))$$.

The first equation means that by a residual gauge transformation, we may transform $\tau$ to any solution of the two-dimensional wave equation.

We can consider $X^\mu(\tau,\sigma)$ (solutions of the two-dimensional wave equation) and $\tau$ can be transformed to be proportional to any one component of $X^\mu$. By doing this in a non-covariant manner, we can solve the Virasoro constraints.

This is the simplest solution to the Einstein’s equations. It is a spherically symmetric vacuum solution. It describes the spacetime outside compact gravitating bodies.

The metric can be written as 

$$ds^2=-e^{2\alpha}dt^2+e^{2\beta}dr^2+r^2(d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta d\phi^2)$$

Note: A metric with a timelike #Killing-Vector is *stationary*. If in addition there are no dtdx cross terms, the metric is *static*.

[[Birkhoff’s Theorem]]

Now, after solving Einstein’s equations, we obtain the Schwarzschild metric (with G=1) $$ds^2=-(1-\frac{2M}{r})dt^2+(1-\frac{2M}{r})^{-1}dr^2+r^2d\Omega^2$$

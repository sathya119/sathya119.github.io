The second Gita, being in the same Raga as the first (and the same Tala), is very similar to [[Gītā 1 - Srī Gaṇanātha]]. This Gita too is in praise of Lord Ganesha.

**Rāgā:** Malahari

Avarohana (Descending) - Ṡ D$_1$ P M$_1$ G$_2$ R$_1$ S

**Language:** Kannaḍā

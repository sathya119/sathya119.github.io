$$\mathcal{L}=\frac{1}{2}\mathring{\phi}^2-\frac{1}{2}(\nabla\phi)^2-\frac{m^2\phi^2}{2}$$
$$\Pi=\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\mathring{\phi}}=\mathring{\phi}$$
$$\mathcal{H}=\Pi^2-\frac{1}{2}\Pi+\frac{1}{2}(\nabla\phi)^2+\frac{m^2\phi^2}{2}$$
$$H=\int d^3x\frac{1}{2}(\Pi^2+(\nabla\phi)^2+m^2\phi^2)$$
Now quantise it using equal time commutation relations,
$$[\phi(x),\phi(y)]_{x^0=y^0}=0=[\Pi(x),\Pi(y)]_{x^0=y^0}$$
$$[\phi(x),\Pi(y)]_{x^0=y^0}=i\delta^3(x-y)$$
we take hbar to be 1 (natural units)

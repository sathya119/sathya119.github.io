We can postulate the existence of an exact SU(Nc) colour symmetry with Nc = 3 colours. Let U be a matrix belonging to the group SU(Nc). Then:

$$\textbf{Special: } det(U)=1$$

$$\textbf{Unitary: }UU^\dagger=U^\dagger U=\mathbb{1}$$

We want to construct mesons and baryons as colour singlets, i.e., they should have no isolated colour charges and transform trivially under the colour group. How?
- Mesons: $$\bar{q}^i q^i\rightarrow\bar{q}^j(U^\dagger)_{ji}U_{ik}q^k=\bar{q}^i q^i$$
- Baryons: $$\epsilon_{ijk}q^iq^jq^k=\underbrace{\epsilon_{ijk}U_{ii'}U_{jj'}U_{kk'}}_{det(U)\epsilon_{i'j'k'}}q^{i'}q^{j'}q^{k'}=\epsilon_{ijk}q^iq^jq^k$$
Many events that take place at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), a high-energy particle collider, are governed by QCD.

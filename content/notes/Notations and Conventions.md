A four component Dirac Fermion with mass M is described by the Lagrangian $$\mathcal{L}_{Dirac}=i\bar{\Psi}_D\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\Psi_D-M\bar{\Psi}_D\Psi_D$$
We use the specific representation of the 4x4 gamma matrices in 2x2 blocks by $$\gamma^\mu=\begin{pmatrix}0&\sigma^\mu\\-\sigma^\mu&0\end{pmatrix}\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\gamma_5=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$$ where $$\sigma^0=\bar{\sigma}^0=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\sigma^1=-\bar{\sigma}^1=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\sigma^2=-\bar{\sigma}^2=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix}\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\sigma^3=-\bar{\sigma}^3=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$$
In this representation, a four-component Dirac spinor is written in terms of 2 two-component complex, anti-commuting objects; $$\Psi_D=\begin{pmatrix}\xi_\alpha\\\chi^{\dagger\dot{\alpha}}\end{pmatrix}$$

Enharmonic notes are those which have the same pitch/tuning, but bear different names.
For example, $$B_{}\flat$$ is enharmonic to $$A\sharp$$.

When we talk about scales from different forms of music, we can use this term to relate them.
For example, the raga “Naṭhabhairavi” is enharmonic to the Natural Minor scale and the raga “Mohanam” is enharmonic to the Major Pentatonic scale.

- **Loops:** each loop gives an integral over the unconstrained loop momentum, k,

$$ \int \frac{d^Dk}{(2\pi)^D}$$ where $$D=4-2\epsilon$$ in Dimensional Regularisation.

- **Closed fermion/ghost loops:** introduce an extra minus sign for each closed fermion and ghost loops.
- **External fermion lines:** introduce a relative negative sign between diagrams with (anti-commuting) fermions interchanged.
- **Symmetry factors:** divide by the number of symmetries of the graph.
- **Dirac algebra:** go against the spin flow when writing an amplitude.

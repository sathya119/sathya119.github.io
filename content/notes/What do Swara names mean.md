- Shadja - Giving birth to six offsprings (name Ri, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni). It is named so because Sa is present in every scale; no scale is permitted to omit this Swara.
- Rishabha - Literally means a bull. It is believed that this Swara originated from the lowing of a bull. Can be related to Nandi, Lord Shiva’s vehicle.
- Gandhara - The literal meaning has no connection to its origin (literally means Land of Fragrance). Ga is believed to have originated from the bleat of a goat. Some people say that Gandhara denotes the Gandharvas who are the singers in heaven.
- Madhyama - The middle one, since its the 4th Swara out of 7.
- Panchama - Fifth
- Dhaivata - Related to God (Devas)
- Nishada - End of a cycle

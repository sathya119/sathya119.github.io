The SM gauge group is based on the following symmetry: $$SU(3)_C\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\;\;\;\overrightarrow {\text{spontaneously broken}} \;\;\;SU(3)_C\times U(1)_{EM}$$

Here, $$U(1)_{EM}$$ couples to the electromagnetic charge $$Q_{EM}=T_{L3}+Y$$ where $$T_{L3}$$ is the weak component of isospin (third generator of $$SU(2)_L$$)

We have 3 generations of fermions: identical gauge quantum number but different masses.

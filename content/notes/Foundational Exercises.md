The names of these exercises are generally in Tamil (although you will find it in other languages too). The word “Varisai” basically means a sequence. Hence, you would find that these exercises are basically sequences of swaras in different orders (check [[What is a Swara]]).

Traditionally, these exercises, when first taught, are done so in a raga called **Mayamalavagowla** (check [[What is a Raga]]), which is called *Bhairav* in Hindustani Music and *Double Harmonic Major Scale* in Western Music (there’s a term for this: [[Enharmony]]).

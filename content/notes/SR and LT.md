The Lorentz group consists of linear transformations Λ that leave $$x^2=x_\mu x^\mu$$ invariant.
They have the form $$x^\mu \longrightarrow x'^\mu=\Lambda^\mu_\nu x^\nu\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;x_\mu\longrightarrow x'_\mu=\Lambda^\nu_\mu x_\nu=x_\nu(\Lambda^{-1})^\nu_\mu$$
For LTs, the norm is invariant so that, $$x^2\rightarrow (x')^2=(x')^TGx'=x^T\Lambda^TG\Lambda x=x^TGx\;\text{ iff }\;\Lambda^TG\Lambda=G$$
The Lorentz group splits into 4 components: $$\text{i) }\Lambda = \mathbb{1}$$$$\text{ii) }\Lambda=-G$$$$\text{i) }\Lambda = -\mathbb{1}$$$$\text{ii) }\Lambda=G$$
	These four elements form a subgroup of dimension 4.

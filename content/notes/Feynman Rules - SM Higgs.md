#### Coupling to Gauge Bosons

For the coupling to W bosons, the relevant term in the Lagrangian density is

$$(D_{\mu}\Phi)^{\dagger}(D^{\mu}\Phi)\supset\frac{1}{4}g^2(v+h)^2W^+_{\mu}W^{-\mu}$$.

Expanding the term in the Higgs field h, we get as the leading term, the W boson mass term $M_W^2=g^2v^2/4$. The hWW and hhWW vertices are

![[hWW.light.svg]] 

$$=i\frac{g^2v}{2}g_{\mu\nu}=2i\frac{M^2_{W}}{v}g_{\mu\nu}$$

![[hhWW.light.svg]] 

$$=i\frac{g^2}{4}\times 2g_{\mu\nu}=2i\frac{M^2_{W}}{v^2}g_{\mu\nu}$$. The extra factor of 2 is a symmetry factor, which accounts for the interchange of two h bosons, since they cannot be distinguished.

For the coupling to Z bosons, the relevant term in the Lagrangian density is,

$$(D_{\mu}\Phi)^{\dagger}(D^{\mu}\Phi)\supset\frac{1}{8}(g^2+g'^2)(v+h)^2W_{\mu}W^{\mu}$$.

Expanding this term in the Higgs field h yields as the leading term, the Z boson mass term, $M_Z^2=(g^2+g’^2)v^2/4$. The hZZ and hhZZ vertices are

![[hZZ.light.svg]]

$$= i\frac{(g^2+g'^2)v}{4}\times 2\times g_{\mu\nu}=2i\frac{M_{Z^2}}{v}g_{\mu\nu}$$

![[hhZZ.light.svg]]

$$= i\frac{(g^2+g'^2)}{8}\times 2\times 2\times g_{\mu\nu}=2i\frac{M_{Z^2}}{v^2}g_{\mu\nu}$$.

The extra factors of 2 and 2x2 are symmetry factors, accounting for the interchange of the two Z bosons and both the two Z bosons and two h bosons since they cannot be distinguished.

#### Coupling to Fermions

For the coupling to quark bosons, the relevant term in the Lagrangian density is

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{Yukawa}}\supset\left[y_{u}\bar{u}_{R}\tilde{\Phi}^{\dagger}Q_{L}+y_{d}\bar{d}_{R}\Phi ^{\dagger}Q_{L}\right]+\text{h.c.}$$

$\tilde\Phi = i\sigma^2\Phi^*$ is the charge conjugate of the Higgs doublet, with hypercharge $Y_{\tilde\Phi}=-1/2$. Moreover, $Q_L$ is the left-handed quark doublet with three generations of quarks, and $u_R$ and $d_{R}$ are the right-handed quark singlets with three generations of up-type and down-type quarks respectively. The quark masses arise as 

$$m_{d,i}=\frac{[y_{d}]_{i}v}{\sqrt{ 2 }},\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;m_{u,i}=\frac{[y_{u}]_{i}v}{\sqrt{ 2 }}$$

where $[y_d]_i$ and $[y_u]_i$ are the Yukawa couplings to the $i^{th}$ generation.

Expanding the term in the Higgs field h yields as the hqq vertex,

![[hqq.light.svg]]

$$= -i\frac{[y_{q}]}{\sqrt{ 2 }}=-i\frac{m_{q}}{v}$$.

But, the above holds good only if the Yukawa couplings are diagonal, which holds in the so-called mass basis. (See CKM matrix - SM)

Note: The couplings to leptons follow the pattern of the up-type quarks.

#### Higgs Self Interactions

The relevant term in the Lagrangian density is

$$V(\Phi)\supset\mu^2(v+h)^2+\lambda(v+h)^4=\lambda v^2h^2+\lambda vh^3+\frac{\lambda}{4}h^4+\text{const}$$.

Expanding this term in the Higgs field h yields as the first term, the Higgs boson mass term $m_h^2=2\lambda v$. The hhh and hhhh vertices can be obtained as

![[hhh.light.svg]]

$$=-i\lambda v\times 3! = -6i\lambda v=-3i\frac{m^2_{h}}{v}$$

![[hhhh.light.svg]]

$$=-i\frac{\lambda}{4}\times 4! =-6i\lambda=-3i\frac{m^2_{h}}{v^2}$$.

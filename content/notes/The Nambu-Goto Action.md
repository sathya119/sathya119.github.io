Let us generalise the world-line formulation of a classical relativistic particle to two-dimensional world-sheets.

Orthonormal coordinates: $$X^\mu=\{X^0, X^1,\dots,X^{D-1}\}$$ on flat D-dimensional #Minkowski spacetime $$\mathbb{R}^{1,D-1}$$.

Coordinates: $$\sigma^a=(\sigma^0,\sigma^1)$$ on the string world-sheet $$\Sigma$$.

Trajectory of a string: $$X: \Sigma\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{1,D-1}$$ with coordinate functions $$X^\mu(\sigma)$$.

In other words,

$$X:\Sigma\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{1,D-1}$$

$$X:\sigma\mapsto X^\mu(\sigma)$$

The natural generalisation on the relativistic action principle is

$$S=-T\int_{\Sigma} d(\text{area})=-T \int_{\Sigma} d^2\sigma\sqrt{-\text{det}(\gamma)}$$ where $$\gamma_{ab} = \partial_{a} X^\mu\partial_{b}X^\nu\eta_{\mu\nu}$$ and $$\partial_{a}X^\mu=\frac{\partial X^\mu}{\partial \sigma^a}$$.

The symmetric tensor $$\gamma_{ab}$$ is metric on $$\Sigma$$ induced by $$X: \Sigma\rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{1,D-1}$$ and the real parameter T>0 is known as the string tension.

The action (known as Nambu-Goto Action) is proportional to the area of the world-sheet and therefore, classical solutions $$X^\mu(\sigma)$$ minimise the world-sheet area.

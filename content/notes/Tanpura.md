This is a Tanpura, a four-stringed, wooden instrument. Depending on the Shruti required, the String thickness varies.

Shrutis are grouped to accommodate usage of the strings for more than one Shruti. They are usually grouped as Gents Pitch (B-D) and Ladies Pitch (G-A#). You would also notice that Male Shruti tanpuras are larger to produce the low frequencies and Female Shruti tanpuras are smaller to produce the higher frequencies.

Nowadays, almost every singer and student has shifted to digital tanpura, either on their devices or what’s called a “Shruti Box” and performers use the tanpura more as a decorative element on stage. Yet, procuring their first tanpura remains the dream for many beginners and aesthetes of Indian Classical Music.

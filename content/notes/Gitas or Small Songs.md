Gitas (lit. songs) are small notation based songs praising different forms of the Lord. The first four (also called Pillari Gitas) are based on a raga called _Malahari_. Beyond these, we find similar Gitas in other “simple” ragas such as _Mohanam_ and _Kalyani_. Note that I have put simple in quotes since I just mean that their scales are easier to understand. As a raga, they are as complex as any other!

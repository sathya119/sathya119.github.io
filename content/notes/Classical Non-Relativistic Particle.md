Let us consider a non-relativistic particle of mass m, moving non-relativistical in a flat (D-1)-dimensional space $$\mathbb{R}^{D-1}$$ with orthonormal coordinates $$\vec{x}=\{x^1,...,x^{D-1}\}$$. The action principle is

$$S=\frac{1}{2}m\int dt\;|\dot {\vec x}|^2$$.

The canonical momenta are $$\vec p=\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial\dot{\vec x}}=m\dot {\vec x}$$ and the energy is $$E=\dot {\vec x}.\vec p-\mathcal{L}=\frac{|\vec p|^2}{2m}$$.

The change in action is $$\delta S=-m\int dt \;\delta\vec x.\ddot{\vec x}=-\int dt \;\delta \vec x.\dot{\vec p}$$. Thus, the equations of motion are $$\dot {\vec p}=0$$.

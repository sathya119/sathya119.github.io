Neutrinos are electrically neutral fermions which are part of the $$SU(2)_L$$ doublet. They undergo weak interactions with force carriers W and Z bosons.

Note: $$m(W^\pm)\sim80\;GeV\;\;\;\;m(Z)\sim91 \; GeV$$

$$\sum_i m_{\nu_i}\lesssim1\;eV$$

Neutrinos in the [[Standard Model]] are described by the Weyl spinors with left chirality: $$P_L=\frac{1}{2}(1-\gamma^5)$$

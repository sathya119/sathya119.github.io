To quantise our theory, we need to input the uncertainty principle. Again, parallels help:

$$[X,P]=i\hbar \implies[\phi,\Pi]=i\hbar\mathbb{1}$$.

Just like we cannot measure momentum and position simultaneously and to arbitrary precision, we cannot determine the field value and its time derivative to arbitrary precision either. So, we promote $\phi,\Pi$ to operators and impose a form that satisfies commutation relations.

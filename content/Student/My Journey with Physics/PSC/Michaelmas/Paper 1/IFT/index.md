First, why Field Theory?
- We need a consistent quantum treatment of relativistic particles.
- Our theory should be local and causal (since nature is).
- Our theory should allow for a dynamical number of particles.

With a QFT (Quantum ...), we can treat all particles on the same footing, with a common framework (spin-statistics relation emerges). It becomes the language to formulate our most fundamental theory of nature. It also supports a consistent, low energy theory for quantum gravity (our next to last theory).

But, how do we build a QFT?
1. **Dynamics**: Review Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics and apply it to fields.
2. **Quantise**: Implement canonical commutation relations.
3. **Interpret**: Introduce Fock Space and identify familiar operators.

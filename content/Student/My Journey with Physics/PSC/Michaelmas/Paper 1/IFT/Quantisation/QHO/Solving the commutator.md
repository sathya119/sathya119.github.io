$$[\phi(\vec{x}),\Pi(\vec{y})]=-i \int[d \vec{k}][d \vec{k'}]AB(e^{i \vec{k}.\vec{x}}e^{-i \vec{k'} \vec{y}}[a_{k},a^{\dagger}_{k'}]+e^{-i \vec{k}.\vec{x}}e^{i \vec{k'} \vec{y}}[a^{\dagger}_{k},a_{k'}])$$

$$=i \int[d \vec{k}]AB(e^{i \vec{k}(\vec{x}-\vec{y})}+\text{h.c.})=i\hbar \delta^{3}(\vec{x}-\vec{y})$$

such that $\frac{2A.B}{N_{k}}=\hbar$ after expanding our notation.

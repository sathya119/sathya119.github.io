**”Regular Schedule”**

05:00-05:20 - Wake-up Bells
05:30-05:50 - Suprabhatam and Morning Prayers
06:00-06:40 - Jogging and Exercises
06:40-07:30 - Games, Practise, etc. (some breathing time xD)
07:45-08:15 - Breakfast
08:30-08:45 - College Bells
08:45-09:00 - College Prayers and Morning Assembly
09:05-12:05 - Lecture Hours
12:35-13:05 - Lunch
13:15-16:15 - Lecture Hours
16:30 - Tea and Snacks
16:30-18:00 - Games, Practise, etc. (some more breathing time)
18:00 - Vedam Chanting
18:30-19:00 - Bhajans and Mangala Arati
19:15-19:50 - Dinner
20:00 - Study Prayers
20:00-21:30 - Study Hours (Strictly at your desk, studying only!)
21:30-21:50 - Milk, Some TV, last window of breathing time
22:00 - Lights off and Night Prayers
Past 22:00 - Everyone sleeps (except we don’t T-T)

A few lucky souls known as the Line Leaders (the Audio-Visual or the AVC claims so too) get the unique opportunity of waking up before everyone at 04:30 and sleeping after everyone at around 22:30.

If you are part of the Music Group, congratulations! Get ready to wake up as early as 03:00 to practise for the various programmes. 

Although I must say, every department has its own early wake-up times for different seasons of the academic year, which I am not going to list here.
